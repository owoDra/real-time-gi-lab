## Phase 1 — establish competitiveness

**Question:** Under valid physical HDR references and a fair end-to-end boundary,
where does the current Renderer win, lose or remain inconclusive?

- Begin from N010's frozen24-slot corpus, complete quality/budget selection,
  dynamic histories, timing-validity and memory procedures. N007/N008/N009
  bounded negative/incomplete results remain closed historical findings.
- The initial executable handoff cohort is incumbent/vanilla RC and pixel PT.
  DDGI/RTXGI is pinned to Vulkan on AMD, without CUDA. Qualify integrated DDGI
  correctness, semantic/quality parity, timing and memory before accepted DDGI
  comparisons in Lane M; SDK/source builds are not runtime GI evidence.
- Compare representative GI families fairly, including supported HWRT and
  software pathways. Retain losses, unsupported regimes, quality-unmatched
  budgets and inconclusive timings. An incumbent win is not required.
- Extend beyond a single Sponza/GPU before any general superiority claim.

**Exit:** retained matched quality/GPU-time/owned-memory/dynamics comparisons,
credible independent reference eligibility, explicit deployment limits and a
ranked list of observed architectural bottlenecks.

## Phase 2 — investigate structural improvements

**Question:** Which transport, representation or reuse change can improve the
observed quality–cost frontier?

- Address the largest demonstrated Phase1 weaknesses; avoid indefinite local
  tuning without a plausible complete-system benefit.
- Split RC, HWRT, pages, hashes, temporal reuse and guiding are replaceable
  techniques. Preserve a measured incumbent while alternatives are evaluated.
- Register falsifiable hypotheses, exact matched-quality/cost boundaries,
  memory, minimum detectable effect and bounded stop criteria before data.

**Exit:** demonstrated end-to-end improvement versus the frozen incumbent,
with its costs and scope, or an explicit negative/partial result.

## Phase 3 — demonstrate reproducible competitiveness

**Question:** Does a practical complete Renderer support the claimed advantage
across representative workloads and GPU classes?

- Include static, camera/disocclusion, lights, rigid, deforming, topology/high
  churn and varied spatial scales where supported.
- Reproduce on several GPU classes and declare HWRT/software differences.
- Deliver an interactive or captured demo, exact configurations, retained raw
  evidence and independently repeatable complete quality/time/peak accounting.

**Exit:** reproducible scoped advantage or an honest qualified failure/tradeoff.
