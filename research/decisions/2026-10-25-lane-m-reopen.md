# 2026-10-25 — Lane M reopened; completion means meeting the goal

Supersedes the status effect of [2026-10-25 Lane M closure](2026-10-25-lane-m-closure.md).
The closure record, its verdict LANE_M_COMPLETE_CHARTER_NOT_MET and every result
it cites remain preserved as historical evidence; nothing is relabelled.

User decisions (2026-10-25):

1. **Lane M is reopened.** "Complete" now means the research goal is achieved,
   not that a final verdict is recorded. The 047 closure criteria (which allowed
   closing with CHARTER_NOT_MET) no longer define Lane M completion.
2. **Multiple-GPU validation is out of scope.** The target is a game-oriented
   real-time GI architecture; multi-GPU setups are not mainstream and are
   costly, so Lane M does not research them. The charter sentence about
   "multiple GPU classes" is not a Lane M completion gate. One-device claims
   must still be stated as one-device claims and must not be generalized.
3. Resumption happens in the next session from the handoff
   [265](../lanes/lane-m-gi-competitiveness/results/265-session-handoff-reopen-2026-10-25.md).

Lane M completion gate (replacing 047 for completion purposes): a novel,
demonstrably competitive real-time GI transport, implemented in an
image-producing GPU pipeline, that on prospectively registered unseen heldouts
(including Sponza-scale and dynamic camera/light/rigid/deforming scenes, with
AS update and history costs charged) meets the fixed HDR gates and shows
calibrated same-quality GPU-time and/or memory superiority, or same-cost
quality superiority, over fair representative families (raw PT, PT +
established reconstruction, NRD, DDGI, ReSTIR GI, RC), with all losses kept.
Gates, thresholds and the calibrated timing protocol (045) are not relaxed.
